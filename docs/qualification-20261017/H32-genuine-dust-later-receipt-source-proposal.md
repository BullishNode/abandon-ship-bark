Source proposal only. No test source, module, build, listing, allocation or execution is created. H32 remains NOT RUN; existing coverage and historical failures are unchanged.

Start with two real normal accounts and a genuine **100,000-sat board**, retaining the native **20,000-sat minimum**. Publicly cosign, register and deliver **1,200 sats to the recipient and 98,800 sats of sender change**. Both leaves exceed P2TR dust; this is two independently signed checkpoints and three signed Arkoor transactions. Cache the exact 1,200-sat signed body as Spendable in the original recipient DB before absence. `sync_mailbox` alone does not prove sender retirement: use the existing public `recover_vtxos([source.id()], None)` control and assert the actual state.

The recipient’s actual registered script must satisfy **200 < script dust**, since 1,200 − 1,000 = 200. That holds for standard P2WPKH (294) and P2TR (330), but the fixture must inspect its real script. At the first real expiry, require gross/forfeit **1,200**, fee/payout **0**, and no recipient output mapping. The real expiry-clause parent and actual anchor child must reach canonical Core 6 confirmations. The sender’s separate 98,800-sat group should pay 97,800 plus its own 1,000-sat charge; the recipient receives no output. Server `Spent` or a completed height is insufficient proof.

Only then reopen the original recipient DB. `sync_fallback_payouts()` must accept **BelowDust** using its full signed path and actual chain proof. Require Successful `below_dust` history, no destination, fee zero, one debit of −1,200 and zero across all ten Ark balance fields. Repeat sync and reopen to preserve the entitlement, movement identity and economic projection.

**After** that terminal proof and accepted history, create a fresh independently funded second donor account, then a genuine 100,000-sat board and signed receipt to a new stored key of the **same account and immutable fallback script**. Do not reuse expired sender change or precreate the second root. Reusing the first donor wallet could coin-select its fallback payout and overlap the two-root cut; a separate later donor prevents that. Hydrate the new receipt in the same recipient DB, assert only 100,000 current principal, and close all live clients again. At the second expiry, require exactly one real recipient payment of **99,000 plus 1,000 fee**, canonical Core 6, with the old forfeiture unchanged. Reject a resurrected **100,200-sat** payment, an extra 1,200 output, predecessor payments or duplicate economic payments across both complete lineages and the postsetup chain/mempool.

Returning history must contain the old dust debit and the new payout debit exactly once across all subsystems: −1,200 and −100,000, relative to captured nonfallback debit baselines. Legitimate receipt credits are separate. Repeat at another fully rebuilt tip and after reopening; require zero Ark buckets and no pending exit. A positive-only helper that unwraps `output_index` cannot serve as the dust-history oracle.

Four genuine 100,000-sat watch sponsors, confirmed before the first expiry, give a declared **600,000-sat selected backing/sponsor cut** with the two boards. Follow actual confirmed child change and unused sponsors; never assume each child consumes a fresh sponsor. Recompute every actual miner fee and account for all terminal descendant leaves. Ordinary board-funding change and upfront board fees sit outside that selected cut and must be labeled separately. No global operator solvency claim follows.
