## Principal current blockers

1. **Canceled internal funding has no autonomous terminal-failure interpretation.** Funding promotion is durable, but source classifier keeps any such funding InternalCommitted even after cancellation and failed attempt. Offline sender refund liveness therefore appears unsatisfied without a returning client or chain resolution.
2. **Signed receive materialization and durable intent are different commitments.** Internal completion before signed materialization can leave source and recipient obligations waiting forever; maintenance only recognizes already fulfilled grants.
3. **Durable external dispatch precedes remote execution.** Interruption in that gap leaves no labeled CLN group and no typed rejection, while redispatch is permanently fenced. Safety is preserved by waiting; eventual recovery is not established.
4. **Stock CLN transport cannot provide required typed no-attempt evidence.** A retained authentic zero-part run fails for this reason. Adapter-positive result does not qualify ordinary stock packaging or changed Nix derivation.
5. **Global hold-settler head-of-line blocking.** An uncollectible/offline external receive retries indefinitely, blocking later unrelated preimages whose inbound HTLC deadlines continue advancing.

### Follow-up: automatic chain progression does not refute L01/L02

Inspected the counter-path after drafting: expiry_ownership.rs:279-280 sets `needs_chain_progress` for an expired unresolved HTLC. expiry_readiness.rs:63-65 converts it to NeedsHtlcProgress; its root loop requests split when no ordinary hold exists. watchman/mod.rs:395-420 broadcasts signed next transactions. This is progress toward physical leaves, not a refund. `request_expiry_split` in database/expiry_payout.rs:243-244 returns false when the root has no `oor_spent_txid`. At a ServerHtlcSend leaf, watchman/policy.rs:191-206 progresses any known signed successor, else claims only with a stored preimage, else Wait. With both clients absent, no preimage and no signed refund successor, the chain path therefore does not establish payment or terminalize canceled internal source funding. Actual automatic split/final-leaf observation is missing runtime evidence, so retain source-supported status rather than observed loss. Depending on actual backing shape, progression can leave only the customer's timeout key as practical recovery, contradicting offline fallback liveness until that customer returns.
