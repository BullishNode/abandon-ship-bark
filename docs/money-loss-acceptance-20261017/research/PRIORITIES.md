# Scenario priorities and next testing phase

The catalogue has **200 event scenarios, 81 concerning Lightning**: **4 observed failures, 58 source-supported risks, 134 hypotheses requiring validation, 4 historical regression risks**. There are 39 P0, 157 P1 and 4 P2 entries. These priorities order later controlled defensive testing; they do not turn conditional scenarios into confirmed defects. No tests or fixes were implemented during this assignment.

## Recommended order for later scenario suites

1. Freeze an exact candidate, executable/plugin/config profile and independent starting liability model. Use existing retained failures as separate baseline cases. Do not relabel old dirty/runtime evidence as a current clean build.
2. Exercise the Lightning terminal matrix first: unused/partially attempted/dispatched sends, zero-part typed errors, actual success, cancellation, internal zero-grant/expired-grant closure, late same-hash success and permanent client absence. Require both correct payment and no extra refund across the full lineage.
3. Exercise recipient continuity: forwarded external/internal receives at each actual durable boundary, including paid intent without materialization, active exit escalation, pool change/ancestors, late registration and mailbox absence. Preserve the original beneficiary independently of provider action state.
4. Exercise atomic financial commitments: refresh/cancel/offboard/transfer versus payout, cross-process admission and registration/scan commits. Use real locks and binding signed facts; do not infer races from sleeps or tear a transaction that is atomic.
5. Exercise physical recovery and funding: extend the successful bounded split shape with real historical ancestry, cap/proof-work limits, unknown sibling, partial exit and sponsor failures. Observe actual Core rejection and confirmation, not broadcast flags.
6. Exercise reorg/restore and client recovery: multiple invalidations, failed/delayed listeners, actual restored keys/contracts, seed-only output discovery and a real spend; separately test false-Spent protection and authenticated-history liveness.
7. Measure funded operational liveness under a declared arrival/history/funding budget. Track oldest unpaid entitlement and contiguous attribution coverage. Synthetic registration counts and scheduled transfers cannot establish production-scale eventual recovery.

Each suite needs an independent per-owner amount/script model, actual Lightning terminal evidence and canonical outpoints, whole-lineage duplicate/value checks and an eventual-progress bound appropriate to the declared environment. Do not claim “passed” from one expected recipient while another branch receives an extra payment.

## Requirements not currently met or not established

**Source-supported gap:** failed/interrupted internal payments can retain `InternalCommitted` without an autonomous absent-client refund path. Normal completion repair does not implement that terminal closure.

**Client contract discrepancy:** K3a's requirement to retain protection on unsupported server-Spent reports is contradicted by the inspected no-entitlement adoption path unless an additional admission guarantee excludes the explicit scenario. The ordinary-spend test does not supply that guarantee.

**Actual compatibility failure:** the inspected Noah backend/provider interface cannot produce the required exact LNURL metadata-hash commitment through its current description-only invoice request. This prevents strict offline receive admission before payment.

**Information limit:** a genuinely unregistered old owner, or hardened child outside the registered xpub's derivation capability, cannot be identified by completing normal registered-xpub searches. Current safe Hold avoids appropriation but does not meet unconditional eventual identification/recovery without more owner evidence. Ark seed alone also cannot recover an unrelated external wallet seed; that is a recovery prerequisite, not an unexplained implementation bug.

**Conditional liveness gaps:** whole-anchor inventory refusal before splitting, unsafe-only split refusal, unresolvable siblings and required signing material lost in a backup can leave legitimate liabilities unpaid. The successful 327-owner fixture does not establish progress for those prerequisites. Exact grouping opportunity, external-address authorization and earlier receive-fee quote rules still need owner clarification where noted.

The most important missing observations are exact permanently absent-client terminal recovery at those boundaries, current artifact/dependency pairing in the actual deployment, real funded attribution under admitted workload, and recovery from authentic historical/backup states. Their absence is recorded so future testing has a defensible target; this research does not wait for them or conduct new tests.
