# H11: forward one principal through three owners

**Source proposal only; unassigned, uncompiled and NOT RUN.** Original H11 is “Forward received value across three owners”; the full oracle is “Final live entitlement is counted once; predecessors cannot receive it again.” No selector, owner or source 20 is reserved.

Start three independently registered ordinary accounts A, B and C with distinct normal keys, mailboxes and authenticated fallback scripts. Only A is funded and boards a real **100,000 sats**, retaining the native **20,000-sat minimum**. Use existing persistent setup wrappers with real SDK clients so original databases can be reopened without a new recovery framework. Assert the unchanged fixture board fee and actual 100k net; ordinary funding change/miner fee are separate.

Use normal `send_arkoor_payment` for the complete A→B amount, then actual `B.sync_mailbox()`. Require the exact 100k full signed B body, own stored key, original backing/expiry and completed signed-path registration; A is Spent with no change and no pending send. Only then B forwards its full receipt to C through the same normal SDK action. Validate C’s genuine 100k body and ancestry through both prior sources; B is spent with no change. C alone holds the live principal. Three devices belonging to two seeds, or transient keys belonging to A, do not meet this requirement.

Capture each complete history and local body/state before stopping all daemons and dropping every live wallet. Let unchanged automatic captaind/watchman expiry settle the original physical root with confirmed watch funds. No refresh, partial transfer, split, HTLC, reorg, ACK gate, manual payout or financial SQL is needed. Manual scheduling only establishes controlled absence; it makes no default-daemon claim.

Require one **99,000-sat C payment plus 1,000 policy charge**, exact owner/mailbox/key/script attribution and canonical Core≥6 parent/actual same-block fee child. Scan all predecessor/ancestor IDs, allocation generations and canonical/mempool outputs: no A or B customer allocation/payment for forwarded principal, no duplicate parent payment. Recompute actual fees and the complete selected **300,000-sat backing/sponsor cut** (100k principal plus two real 100k fee-wallet outputs), including unused sponsor outputs and actual confirmed child change. Unknown inflows or omitted outputs fail; the policy charge is not also a miner fee.

Return the original A/B/C databases only after chain settlement. C must accept one verified confirmed receipt/history group with gross 100k, fee 1k, net 99k and one fallback debit −100k; all ten Ark buckets become zero. A/B must retain their successful ordinary sends and spent full bodies, without additional fallback debits, payments or revived principal. Compare stable all-subsystem economic maps to preabsence baselines. A’s ordinary send, B’s ordinary send and C’s fallback legitimately debit three different owners; summing them as duplicate economic payment would be a false oracle. Repeat sync, another completed rebuilt tip and reopen before final acceptance.

Proposed bounds are three actors, one Core, no pool/LN, one 40-block lifetime/exit delta 2, existing one-second harness watch cadence, default 180 seconds, retries 0 and at most 96 progress blocks. These are unmeasured source-design inputs. Exact expiry and completed-view barriers, not sleeps or height alone, govern acceptance. Two sponsors are a bounded funding proposal, not proof of actual readiness.
